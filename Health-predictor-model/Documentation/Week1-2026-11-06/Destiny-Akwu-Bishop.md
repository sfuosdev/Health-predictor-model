# GitHub Issues:
**GitHub Issues are a way to capture anything that needs attention in the project. From tasks, to bugs, and even ideas. It is an essential GitHub toll for staying on task and organized. They are used to track task by monitoring all the changes, roles and responsibility, and putting them in one easy to see place.**

# GitHub Milestones:
**Milestones are a great way to track progress and stay on tasks. They are a crucial part of tracking and can help keep everyone on task. Milestones organize related issues by allowing members to see when certain issues or parts of issues are due and what responsibilities they may have.**

# Labels and Assigness:
**Labels allow you to describe the type of theme the issue is based upon. Assignees are the people or person, certain issues or tasks are assigned towards. They help organize team responsibilties, as each member of the team now knows what tasks they have been assigned to, their due date, and what is being expected of them.**
