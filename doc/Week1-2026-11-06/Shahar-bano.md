## GitHub Basics

**GitHub Issues:** It’s an integrated tool for organizing workflows and keeping track of bug reports, feature requests, and ideas. 

**GitHub Milestones:** Groups tasks by goal or deadline to track progress.

**Labels:** Categorizes issues (e.g. bug)

**Assignees:** Indicate who is responsible for said issue
