# Week 2 Dataset Review
## Dataset 1: CAIR-CVD-2025

**Source** : Mendeley Data

**Number of Records** : 1,529

**Number of Features** : 21

**Prediction Target** : CVD Risk Level (a classification of each patient’s cardiovascular disease risk).

**Link**: [CAIR-CVD-2025](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/d9scg7j8fp/1)

## Dataset 2: CDC Indicators of Heart Disease

**Source** : Kaggle

**Number of Records** : 400k+

**Number of Features** : 17

**Prediction Target** : HeartDisease (Yes or No: whether the respondent has had coronary heart disease or a heart attack), predicted from 17 survey-based features such as BMI, smoking, age category, diabetes, and general health.

**Link**: [CDC Indicators of Heart Disease](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/kamilpytlak/personal-key-indicators-of-heart-disease?resource=download)

# Comparison
## Advantages

Dataset 1 from Mendeley Data has more features (21 inputs) with detailed clinical measurements. Data comes from hospital samples instead of a survey along with a numerical risk score.

Dataset 2 from Kaggle has a huge dataset (400k) making it useful for training. It's also cleaned up according to the website making it easier to use from the start.

## Disadvantanges

Dataset 1 is pretty limited in the size of its patients.

Dataset 2 relies on a survey along with some yes or no answers instead of numerical data.

## Recommendation

I would recommend the first dataset (Mendeley Data) since 1500 isn't too small and the measurements and inputs are more detailed/meticulous.
